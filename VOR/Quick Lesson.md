VOR supplies bearing relative to a ground station and remains a useful conventional navigation and backup source on corporate aircraft.

## Operation

The receiver determines the phase relationship between reference and variable signals to calculate the radial. VOR uses assigned frequencies within 108.00–117.95 MHz, sharing part of the band with ILS localizer channels. A radial is measured outward from the station. Modern receivers send deviation and validity information to electronic displays and flight guidance.

## Signal Flow

```text
VOR ground station --> aircraft antenna --> navigation receiver --> EFIS and flight guidance.
```

## Testing

Weak or intermittent indications may originate in RF coverage, antenna paths, receiver tuning or data interfaces. Compare identifier reception, receiver status and displayed source selection using approved test equipment.

Use applicable aircraft and equipment instructions for actual tests and limits.

## Knowledge Check

What is a VOR radial?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

A bearing extending outward from the VOR ground station, referenced to its assigned orientation (normally magnetic north).

</details>
