Airborne radio telephone communication via a satellite is usually abbreviated to the term [SATCOM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SATCOM).

Satellite communications support voice, operational data links and passenger broadband, depending on equipment and service. Installations may include a satellite data unit, RF electronics and an omnidirectional or steerable antenna. Conventional geostationary networks have limited polar coverage; LEO networks such as Iridium can provide high-latitude service. Passenger internet is not automatically an approved safety-service bearer.

## Aircraft Satellite Communication

### Voice Communications

Satellite voice installations route cockpit or cabin audio through the installed terminal and satellite network. Calling and service capabilities depend on the equipment and provider.

### Data Link Communications

A communications management function can route supported operational messages over SATCOM, VHF or HF data links. FANS 1/A can use satellite communications for CPDLC and ADS-C where supported. Local audio or network operation does not establish satellite registration or message delivery.

### Testing

Distinguish local audio/data interfaces, terminal registration, antenna visibility, RF path and service availability. Use approved test modes and observe RF safety precautions.
