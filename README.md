# DuncanU

Open-source training resources for corporate aircraft avionics technicians, focusing on civilian business jets and corporate turboprops manufactured from 2006 onward. Mature technology remains relevant where fitted to supported aircraft. Legacy-only equipment is background material rather than a core training subject.

The lessons emphasize theory of operation, equipment, signal flow, installation, testing and troubleshooting. They are introductory technical references, not aircraft-specific test procedures.
