VHF voice radios provide line-of-sight air-ground communications. Modern corporate flight decks commonly integrate radio tuning and audio selection.

## Operation

Civil aviation voice channels occupy the VHF communications band near 118–137 MHz and normally use amplitude modulation and alternating transmit/receive (simplex, half-duplex operation). Channel spacing varies by region and equipment. A transceiver shares an antenna for transmit and receive. Audio systems provide microphone audio, push-to-talk and received audio routing.

## Signal Flow

```text
Microphone and PTT --> audio system --> VHF transceiver <--> antenna <--> ground station.
```

## Testing

A receiver can work while its transmit audio, PTT or RF output fails. Separate audio routing, tuning, radio power and RF path problems. Use approved RF loads or test equipment when required.

Use applicable aircraft and equipment instructions for actual tests and limits.

## Knowledge Check

Why is a functioning receiver not proof that the transmitter works?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

The transmit chain has separate keying, audio modulation and RF output functions.

</details>
